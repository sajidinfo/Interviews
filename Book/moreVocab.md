Iske jagah aur bhi verbs use kar sakte ho, jo situation pe depend karta hai.

### Similar Verbs for "Acknowledge"

1. **Accept** – maan lena

   * “He accepted his mistake.”
   * (Usne apni galti maan li.)

2. **Admit** – iqraar karna

   * “She admitted the truth.”
   * (Usne sach maan liya.)

3. **Recognize** – pehchanna / maan lena

   * “The company recognized his efforts.”
   * (Company ne uski mehnat ko maana.)

4. **Confirm** – tasdeeq karna / pakka karna

   * “The manager confirmed the meeting.”
   * (Manager ne meeting confirm ki.)

5. **Appreciate** – shukriya kehna / sarahna

   * “I really appreciate your help.”
   * (Main tumhari madad ki sach me qadar karta hoon.)

6. **Confess** – kabool karna (zyada tar negative ya guilt ke liye)

   * “He confessed his crime.”
   * (Usne apna jurm kabool kiya.)

7. **Respond** – jawaab dena (jab kisi cheez ko acknowledge karna ho)

   * “He didn’t respond to my email.”
   * (Usne meri email ka jawab nahi diya.)

Achha sawaal bhai 👌
**assume, suppose, agree** ye bhi kabhi-kabhi **acknowledge** ki tarah lagte hain, lekin inka meaning alag context me hota hai. Main desi style me samjhata hoon:

---

### 1. **Acknowledge**

👉 Matlab: *Maan lena / accept karna / tasleem karna / shukriya kehna*

* “He acknowledged his mistake.”
  (Usne apni galti maan li.)
* “I acknowledge your support.”
  (Main tumhari madad ko manta hoon.)

---

### 2. **Assume**

👉 Matlab: *Maan lena bina proof ke / andaza lagana*

* “I assumed he was busy.”
  (Maine maan liya tha ki wo busy hai, bina puchhe.)

⚠️ Difference: Acknowledge = sach maan lena, Assume = bas andaza lagana.

---

### 3. **Suppose**

👉 Matlab: *Maan lena for the sake of discussion / hypothetical sochna*

* “Suppose you win the lottery, what will you do?”
  (Maan lo tum lottery jeet gaye, to kya karoge?)

⚠️ Difference: Suppose = imagine karna, Acknowledge = accept karna.

---

### 4. **Agree**

👉 Matlab: *Maan jana / sehmat hona / haan karna*

* “I agree with your opinion.”
  (Main tumhari baat se sehmat hoon.)

⚠️ Difference: Agree = kisi ki baat se sehmat hona, Acknowledge = bas sach ya fact maan lena.

---

### Chhota Summary

* **Acknowledge** = sach / fact / galti ko maan lena.
* **Assume** = bina proof ke andaza lagana.
* **Suppose** = imagine karna / maan lo hypothetically.
* **Agree** = kisi ki baat se sehmat hona.
