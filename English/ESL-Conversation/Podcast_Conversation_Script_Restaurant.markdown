# Podcast Conversation Script: Restaurant
## Episode 02: Hosted by Sajid, Featuring Gulista

**Intro Music Plays (Lively, upbeat tune)**

**Sajid**:  
Welcome back, everyone, to *Friends Talking Life*! I’m your host, Sajid, and I’m pumped to be here with my awesome friend, Gulista, for our second episode. Gulista, what’s good?

**Gulista**:  
Hey, Sajid! I’m ready to spice things up today—pun intended! How about you?

**Sajid**:  
Haha, nice one! I’m starving just thinking about today’s topic—restaurants! We’re diving into our favorite spots, food memories, and maybe some dining disasters. So, Gulista, when you hear “restaurant,” what’s the first place that pops into your mind?

**Gulista**:  
Oh, easy. There’s this little family-run Italian place near my old neighborhood. It’s called Mama Rosa’s, and it’s got this cozy vibe—checkered tablecloths, candles in old wine bottles, the works. Their lasagna is like a hug on a plate. What about you?

**Sajid**:  
That sounds like heaven! For me, it’s this hole-in-the-wall biryani joint my dad took me to as a kid. It’s not fancy—no menus, just a chalkboard with two options: chicken or mutton. But the biryani? Out of this world. It’s like every bite teleports you to spice paradise.

**Gulista**:  
Okay, now I’m hungry! So, what is it about that place that keeps you going back? The food or the memories?

**Sajid**:  
Both, honestly. The food’s amazing, but it’s also the nostalgia. My dad and I would sit there, splitting a giant plate, talking about cricket or school. It was our thing. What about Mama Rosa’s? What keeps you hooked?

**Gulista**:  
It’s the people. The owner, Rosa herself, always remembers my order—lasagna with extra cheese—and she’ll chat with you like you’re family. Plus, they play this old-school Italian music that makes you feel like you’re in some romantic movie. It’s the whole experience.

**Sajid**:  
Love that vibe! Okay, let’s talk about the flip side. Ever had a restaurant experience that was a total disaster?

**Gulista**:  
Oh, yes. So, I went to this trendy new place with friends—it was all over social media, super hyped. We wait an hour for a table, another hour for food, and when it finally comes, my “gourmet” burger is basically a sad patty on a soggy bun. To top it off, the waiter spilled soda on my lap and didn’t even apologize!

**Sajid**:  
No way! That’s like a comedy of errors. Did you guys say anything?

**Gulista**:  
We tried, but the manager was like, “That’s just how we do things.” Never went back. Your turn—what’s your worst restaurant story?

**Sajid**:  
Alright, picture this: I’m on a date, trying to impress, so I pick this fancy seafood place. I order lobster because, you know, I’m feeling classy. Turns out, I’m allergic to shellfish—didn’t know that at the time. Halfway through, my face swells up like a balloon. My date was sweet about it, but I was mortified. Had to rush to the ER mid-date!

**Gulista**:  
Oh my gosh, Sajid! That’s next-level! Did you ever go back to that restaurant?

**Sajid**:  
Heck no! I stick to chicken now. Lesson learned. So, let’s switch gears—what’s a restaurant dish you’d love to master at home?

**Gulista**:  
That lasagna from Mama Rosa’s, hands down. I’ve tried, but I can’t get the sauce right—it’s like they’ve got some secret ingredient. Maybe love? Haha. What about you?

**Sajid**:  
Definitely the biryani from that joint. I’ve watched my mom make it, but mine never has that smoky, layered flavor. I think it’s the chef’s magic touch or maybe the ancient tandoor they use. One day, I’ll crack it.

**Gulista**:  
We need a cooking episode where we try to make these dishes! Okay, what’s the most memorable meal you’ve had at a restaurant—not just the food, but the moment?

**Sajid**:  
Good one. It was my 21st birthday. My family took me to this rooftop restaurant with a view of the city skyline. The food was great—steak, mashed potatoes, the works—but what made it was everyone being there, toasting to me, sharing stories. It felt like a movie moment. You got one like that?

**Gulista**:  
Yeah, it was my graduation dinner. My parents took me to this fancy Indian restaurant. I remember the butter chicken was amazing, but what got me was my dad giving this heartfelt speech about how proud he was. I’m not a crier, but I was tearing up over my naan.

**Sajid**:  
That’s so sweet! Those are the moments that stick with you. Alright, Gulista, before we wrap, what’s your go-to restaurant pick for a night out—someplace you’d take me if I was visiting?

**Gulista**:  
I’m taking you to Mama Rosa’s, no question. We’re splitting a lasagna and tiramisu, and you’re gonna love Rosa’s stories. Where are you taking me?

**Sajid**:  
Straight to that biryani spot. We’ll order a mountain of chicken biryani, grab some mango lassi, and just soak in the chaos of the place. Deal?

**Gulista**:  
Deal! I’m already craving it. Alright, Sajid, this was a blast.

**Sajid**:  
Totally! Thanks for joining me, Gulista. To our listeners, we want to hear about your favorite restaurants or epic dining fails—drop them in the comments or send us a message. That’s it for this episode of *Friends Talking Life*. Catch you next time!

**Outro Music Plays (Warm, cheerful tune)**
