# Podcast Episode 06: Past Accident - Conversation Script

**Intro Music: Upbeat, nostalgic tune fades in and out**

**Sajid**: (cheerful) Welcome back to *The Sajid & Gulista Show*, where we dive into the wild, the weird, and the wonderfully real moments of our lives! I’m your host, Sajid, and as always, I’m joined by my best pal, the one and only—

**Gulista**: (interrupting, playful) Gulista, the star of the show! Sorry, Sajid, had to steal the spotlight there. (laughs)

**Sajid**: (mock offense) Steal the spotlight? Gulista, I’m the sun, you’re just a very shiny moon. (chuckles) Anyway, folks, today’s episode is number six, and we’re getting a bit serious—or at least, as serious as we can get without losing our vibe. The theme is *Past Accident*. We’re digging into a moment that shook us up, literally and figuratively.

**Gulista**: Oh yeah, this one’s a doozy. Sajid, you know exactly which accident we’re talking about, right? The one that left us with stories to tell and maybe a few scars to show off.

**Sajid**: (teasing) Scars? Gulista, you call that tiny scratch a scar? I’ve got paper cuts more dramatic than that! (laughs) But seriously, let’s set the scene for our listeners. Picture this: it’s a few years back, we’re young, reckless, and definitely not as wise as we are now—well, at least *I* am wiser now.

**Gulista**: (sarcastic) Oh, please, Mr. Wisdom. You still try to open soda cans with your teeth. (laughs) But okay, let’s take it back. It was that summer, right? We were out on that road trip, feeling like we owned the world.

**Sajid**: Yup, the infamous road trip of… what was it, 2018? We were cruising down that winding road, music blasting—probably some terrible pop song you insisted on playing.

**Gulista**: (defensive) Excuse me, that was *classic* pop, thank you very much. Anyway, we were in your beat-up old car—what was it, a 2005 sedan that sounded like it was coughing up a lung?

**Sajid**: (laughing) Hey, don’t diss my ride! That car had character. And it got us places… until, well, it didn’t. (pauses) So, let’s get to the accident. It was late afternoon, sun’s dipping low, and we’re on this quiet road. I’m driving, you’re in charge of snacks and the playlist—

**Gulista**: (interrupting) And I was doing an *excellent* job, if I do say so myself. Until, you know, you decided to play racecar driver.

**Sajid**: (groaning) Oh, come on! I wasn’t *that* reckless. Okay, listeners, here’s what happened. We’re going around a curve, and I might’ve been going a *tad* faster than I should’ve. Then, out of nowhere, this deer—Bambi’s cousin—leaps onto the road.

**Gulista**: (dramatic) A deer! It was like something out of a movie. I screamed, you swerved, and next thing I know, we’re off the road, sliding into a ditch. My chips went flying, Sajid!

**Sajid**: (laughing) I forgot about the chips! There were potato crumbs *everywhere*. But yeah, it was chaos. The car skidded, we hit the ditch, and the airbag punched me in the face like I owed it money.

**Gulista**: (giggling) I didn’t get an airbag, just a seatbelt burn and a front-row seat to your dramatic flailing. (serious tone) But honestly, that moment was terrifying. Everything happened so fast. One second we’re singing, the next we’re in a ditch, wondering if we’re okay.

**Sajid**: Yeah, it was a wake-up call. I remember sitting there, heart pounding, checking if you were okay. You were just staring at me, like, “Sajid, what did you *do*?”

**Gulista**: (laughing) Because it was your fault! But no, seriously, we were lucky. The car was totaled, but we walked away with just bruises and that one scratch I’m calling a scar. (pauses) It could’ve been so much worse.

**Sajid**: (reflective) It really could’ve. I think that’s what hit me hardest afterward. Like, we were joking about chips and playlists, but when the tow truck came and we were sitting on the side of the road, it sank in. We were *this close* to something really bad.

**Gulista**: Totally. I remember calling my mom, and she was freaking out, but I was just like, “We’re fine, just… don’t ask about the car.” (laughs) What about you? You had to deal with your dad’s reaction to losing his precious sedan.

**Sajid**: (groaning) Oh, don’t remind me. He gave me that look—you know, the one that says, “I’m not mad, I’m just disappointed”? That was worse than the crash. (chuckles) But you know what? That accident taught me a lot. Like, slow down—literally and in life. I was always rushing back then.

**Gulista**: (teasing) And you’re *so* chill now, right? (serious) But yeah, it made me think about how fragile things are. One minute you’re living your best life, the next you’re in a ditch, picking potato chips out of your hair.

**Sajid**: (laughing) Life lessons with a side of snacks. But for real, it changed how I look at things. I’m more careful now, not just with driving, but with decisions. Like, I think twice before doing something impulsive.

**Gulista**: (nodding) Same. It also made me appreciate our friendship more. We went through that together, and we came out stronger. Like, who else would I want to crash with? (laughs)

**Sajid**: (mock offense) Crash with? You make it sound like I planned it! But yeah, having you there kept me grounded. We were freaking out, but we were freaking out *together*. That’s what counts.

**Gulista**: (warmly) Aww, Sajid, you’re getting soft on me. (to audience) Listeners, don’t let him fool you—he’s a big teddy bear. (serious) But yeah, that accident was a reminder to stay connected, check in on each other, and not take things for granted.

**Sajid**: Well said, Gulista. So, to our listeners, what’s *your* “past accident” story? Maybe it’s a literal crash like ours, or maybe it’s a moment that shook you up and changed your perspective. Hit us up on social media with your stories—we’re @SajidAndGulista on X. We’d love to hear from you!

**Gulista**: (excited) Oh, and if your story involves flying snacks, extra points! (laughs) Alright, folks, that’s it for this episode of *The Sajid & Gulista Show*. Join us next time for more chaos, laughs, and maybe fewer car crashes.

**Sajid**: (chuckling) Fingers crossed. Thanks for tuning in, everyone. Stay safe, drive slow, and keep your chips secure. See you next time!

**Outro Music: Upbeat tune fades in and out**

**Gulista**: (whispering as music fades) And maybe don’t let Sajid drive.

**Sajid**: (laughing) I heard that!

**End of Episode**
