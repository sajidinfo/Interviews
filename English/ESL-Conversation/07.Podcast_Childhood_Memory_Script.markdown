# Podcast Episode 07: Childhood Memory - Conversation Script

**Intro Music: Warm, nostalgic melody with a playful vibe fades in and out**

**Sajid**: (excited) Welcome back to *The Sajid & Gulista Show*, where we unpack the wild, the wacky, and the wonderfully weird moments of our lives! I’m your host, Sajid, and as always, I’ve got my partner-in-crime, the queen of storytelling—

**Gulista**: (interrupting, dramatic) That’s me, Gulista, the *real* star of this show! (laughs) Sorry, Sajid, you know I can’t resist a grand entrance.

**Sajid**: (mock exasperation) Grand entrance? You’re practically stealing the mic! (chuckles) Alright, folks, welcome to episode seven, where we’re taking a trip down memory lane. Today’s theme is *Childhood Memory*, and oh boy, do we have some stories to share.

**Gulista**: (nostalgic) Oh, Sajid, this one’s gonna be fun. Childhood memories are like little time capsules, aren’t they? One minute you’re a kid with no worries, the next you’re telling the world about the time you got stuck in a tree.

**Sajid**: (teasing) Stuck in a tree? Is *that* the story you’re going with? (laughs) I was thinking we’d talk about that one time we thought we were master chefs and nearly burned down your kitchen.

**Gulista**: (gasping) Oh no, you’re bringing *that* up? Okay, listeners, buckle up, because this story is pure chaos. Picture this: it’s, what, 2005? We’re like ten years old, and we decide we’re gonna be the next big thing in cooking.

**Sajid**: (grinning) Yup, we were convinced we could make the world’s best pizza. Not just any pizza—*gourmet* pizza, with every topping we could find in your mom’s pantry.

**Gulista**: (laughing) Gourmet? Sajid, we dumped a whole jar of pickles on that thing! And don’t forget the chocolate syrup. We thought we were culinary geniuses.

**Sajid**: (chuckling) Geniuses? More like mad scientists. So, here’s the scene: we’re in Gulista’s kitchen, flour everywhere, looking like a snowstorm hit. I’m tossing dough like I’m on a cooking show, and you’re raiding the fridge like it’s a treasure hunt.

**Gulista**: (giggling) I remember your face when you found that jar of olives. You were like, “This is it, this is what makes it fancy!” (pauses) But then we got to the oven part, and that’s where things went… let’s say, *toasty*.

**Sajid**: (dramatic) Toasty is putting it lightly! We cranked the oven to max because we thought “hotter means faster.” Next thing we know, there’s smoke billowing out, the fire alarm’s screaming, and your mom comes running in like we’re arsonists.

**Gulista**: (laughing) Oh my gosh, her face! She was so mad, but also trying not to laugh because we looked so ridiculous—covered in flour, holding this sad, charred pizza. I think we cried more over the pizza than the fact we almost set off a full-on fire.

**Sajid**: (reflective) Yeah, but you know what? That memory sticks with me because it was so… us. We were fearless, dreaming big, even if our big dream was a pickle-covered disaster. (chuckles) It’s like, as kids, we didn’t care about failing—we just went for it.

**Gulista**: (nodding) Totally. That’s what I love about childhood memories. They’re so pure. Like, we weren’t worried about what anyone thought. We just wanted to make the best pizza ever. (pauses) It makes me a little sad, you know? Growing up means overthinking everything.

**Sajid**: (thoughtful) You’re right. Back then, a mistake was just a funny story. Now, I’d probably stress about the cost of the ingredients we ruined! (laughs) But that day taught me something—having fun with your best friend is worth a little chaos.

**Gulista**: (teasing) A *little* chaos? Sajid, we were banned from the kitchen for a month! (serious) But yeah, it’s those moments that make me grateful for our friendship. We’ve been making messes together since we were kids, and we’re still at it.

**Sajid**: (warmly) Messes and memories, that’s our brand. (to audience) So, Gulista, what’s another childhood memory that pops into your head? Got any solo adventures, or are all your good stories with me?

**Gulista**: (playful) Oh, I had a life before you, Sajid! (pauses) Okay, here’s one. When I was eight, I decided I was going to be a superhero. I tied a bedsheet around my neck like a cape and jumped off the porch, thinking I could fly.

**Sajid**: (gasping) No way! Did you actually think you’d soar like Superman?

**Gulista**: (laughing) Yup! Spoiler: I didn’t fly. I landed in the bushes and sprained my ankle. But for that one second in the air, I felt invincible. (reflective) It’s funny how kids believe anything’s possible.

**Sajid**: (grinning) That’s amazing. I can just see little Gulista, cape flapping, diving into a bush. (pauses) For me, it’s gotta be the time I built a “spaceship” out of cardboard boxes. I was so sure I’d blast off to Mars. Spoiler: I just sat in the garage for hours, making rocket noises.

**Gulista**: (giggling) Rocket noises? Please tell me you’ve got a recording of that somewhere. (to audience) Listeners, this is why we’re friends—we’re both a little ridiculous. But those childhood dreams, they shape you, right? They remind you to keep imagining, even when life gets serious.

**Sajid**: (nodding) Exactly. Those memories are like little sparks that keep us going. (to audience) So, what’s *your* favorite childhood memory? Did you try to fly like Gulista or build a spaceship like me? Maybe you also ruined a perfectly good pizza? Hit us up on X at @SajidAndGulista—we want to hear your stories!

**Gulista**: (excited) Oh, and if your story involves a homemade superhero costume, you’re automatically our favorite. (laughs) Alright, folks, that’s it for episode seven of *The Sajid & Gulista Show*. Thanks for joining us on this nostalgia trip!

**Sajid**: (cheerful) Yeah, thanks for tuning in! Keep dreaming big, stay out of the bushes, and maybe don’t put pickles on your pizza. See you next time!

**Outro Music: Nostalgic melody fades in and out**

**Gulista**: (whispering as music fades) And don’t let Sajid near an oven.

**Sajid**: (laughing) Oh, come on! I’m a master chef now!

**End of Episode**
